# Ocean: Brand Identity & Guidelines v1.0
## The "Silent Luxury" Marketplace Paradigm

This document establishes the strategic, verbal, and visual guidelines for the **Ocean** brand. Every screen, label, illustration, color selection, and UI transition must support these specifications to ensure Ocean feels like an authentic, cohesive, premium global brand.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Personality

Ocean is not an online warehouse or a standard storefront. Ocean is a **curated digital vessel for premium craftsmanship**. Our brand voice represents quiet confidence, editorial curation, and technological sophistication.

| Dimension | Ocean is... | Ocean is NOT... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Restrained, objective, clear | Loud, hype-driven, commercial |
| **Visuals** | Negative space, high contrast, elegant typography | Gradients, heavy drop shadows, neon accents |
| **Philosophy** | Every pixel earns its place | Cluttered, decorative, noisy |
| **UX Feel** | Frictionless, predictable, high-speed | Game-ified, high-friction, confusing |

---

## 2. Core Brand Values

1. **Craftsmanship Over Volume:** We believe products are investments. We prioritize merchant curation, high-fidelity media, and deep technical details over endless low-quality catalogs.
2. **Confidence Through Clarity:** A premium customer does not need neon "SALE" badges, artificial countdown timers, or popups. We build trust through absolute pricing transparency, authenticated reviews, and clear estimated delivery dates (EDD).
3. **Technological Craft:** Luxury and advanced technology are not mutually exclusive. Ocean integrates conversational AI (semantic search, review analysis) seamlessly into the layout so that it feels handcrafted, human-curated, and completely intuitive.

---

## 3. Logo Concept & Strict Rules

The Ocean logo represents a calm, deep tide: a structured horizontal line paired with a minimalist sans-serif logotype.

```
                  O  C  E  A  N
              ───────────────────
```

### Logo Rules
* **Exclusion Zone:** Keep a minimum clear space of `2.0rem` (32px) around all sides of the logotype. No text, imagery, or border lines may intrude on this buffer.
* **Proportions:** The logotype should always maintain a horizontal ratio.
* **Colors:** Allowed configurations are restricted to:
  - Deep Navy on Alabaster Background (Standard Light Mode)
  - Pure Alabaster on Charcoal Background (Standard Dark Mode)
* **Prohibited Modifications:**
  - Never add gradients to the logotype.
  - Never rotate or warp the logo.
  - Never use drop shadows or decorative filters.

---

## 4. Brand Typography System

Ocean utilizes three distinct typefaces to convey a technical, modern editorial feel:

1. **Primary Interface Font: Inter (Sans-Serif)**
   - Used for structural elements, inputs, buttons, dense list items, and standard copy.
   - Values legibility, robust spacing characteristics, and clean vertical metrics.
2. **Display & Heading Font: Space Grotesk (Tech Sans-Display)**
   - Used for primary page titles, category hero sections, and section headers.
   - Infuses Ocean with a clean, tech-forward, modern aesthetic.
3. **Data & Metadata Font: JetBrains Mono (Slab Monospace)**
   - Used for SKUs, order tracking IDs, currency pricing, delivery times, and tech specs.
   - Evokes a sense of absolute precision and digital reliability.

## 5. Visual Asset Guidelines

### A. Photography & Media
* **Subject Treatment:** Focus heavily on natural lighting, raw materials, and sharp depth of field. Backgrounds must be neutral (off-whites, soft grays, deep slates) to let product colors pop naturally.
* **Composition:** Center products with generous padding (at least 15% inner canvas border spacing) to signal high-end luxury. Avoid stock photos with corporate-smiling people. Focus instead on products being used in clean, minimalist, human-occupied environments.
* **Aspect Ratio:** All product listings, details pages, and category headers must maintain a fixed **4:3 aspect ratio** to enforce grid consistency.

### B. Icons & Illustrations
* **Icon Engine:** Exclusively use line-based icons (preferably Lucide) with a strict stroke width of `1.5px`.
* **Filled vs. Outline:** Use outlined icons by default. Only transition to a filled icon to denote an active selection (e.g., a filled wishlist heart or filled cart indicator).
* **Illustration Strategy:** Avoid cartoonish, tech-startup illustrations. Use instead technical wireframes, structural schematics, or clean vector silhouettes to communicate concepts.

## 6. Tone of Voice & Writing Guidelines

The Ocean copywriter avoids buzzwords, exclamation marks, and generic marketing copy. We write with clean, direct, and helpful clarity.

### Examples of Core Content Writing:

| Context | Prohibited (Standard/AI-like) | Required (Ocean Premium Standard) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Empty Cart** | "Your cart is crying! 😭 Hurry up and add some awesome items to check out!" | "Your shopping cart is currently empty. Begin exploring our curated collections to select premium pieces." |
| **Checkout Success** | "Woohoo! Your order is placed! 🎉 Shipping is underway, get excited!" | "Order successfully received. We have sent a detailed confirmation invoice to your email with logistics details." |
| **Error Handling** | "Oopsie! Something went wrong! Please try again!" | "A connection timeout occurred while updating your payment method. Please check your credentials and retry." |
| **Discount/Coupon** | "BAM! 20% OFF! LIMITED TIME ONLY! HURRY UP!" | "Complementary curation discount applied. 20% has been deducted from your final subtotal." |
